# Release 4 — Player-controlled days and encounter recovery

Travel duration stays unchanged. Gather, fish and hunt: 20 minutes. Search objects and refill bottles: 10 minutes. Craft, build, upgrade, treat injuries, complete fights and restore the beacon: 30 minutes. Talking, equipping and assigning chores: no time.

The clock now stores whole hours plus minutes. Older saves receive minute: 0. Resource needs advance proportionally, and midnight still runs daily production, fire use, seasons and gathering refresh once.

No forced midnight sleep or automatic 10 PM return. Sunset warns that night travel and gathering cost 50% more energy; awake nighttime fatigue drains 2 energy per hour. Energy-dependent work stops when too tired, but returning to camp remains available. Rest choices are 30 minutes, 1 hour or until dawn; full sleep ends at the next 6:00 AM. The prominent end-day button returns home using normal travel duration before sleeping. Survival needs continue during rest, so carry food and water. Rough rest is available without a shelter, with less recovery.

A persistent Investigate nearby person action opens an unresolved stranger interaction even if its popup was lost. Active combat likewise has a Return to the encounter action. Stranger meetings triggered while searching now open the interaction immediately. Other work stays blocked until the person or threat is addressed.

111 tests pass, including the Far Shore campaign and new timing, rest, save migration and encounter-recovery cases. Desktop browser checks verified the minute clock, a thirty-minute rest and waking at 6 AM. Phone hardware testing has not been performed.

Use index.html. Older standalone pages still run the legacy engine.
